# BUG-101: Customers can't cancel orders that are packed

**Reporter:** support team
**Severity:** medium

Customers report that the "Cancel order" button is rejected once their order is in the
warehouse ("Packed"), even though it has not shipped yet. They expected to be able to cancel.

Steps:
1. Place an order as customer c-1 and pay for it.
2. Warehouse marks it PACKED.
3. Customer c-1 tries to cancel -> rejected.

Notes from the developer on call: "I think packed orders should be cancellable but maybe only
before the courier label is printed? Not sure." (This is an opinion, not an approved rule.)
