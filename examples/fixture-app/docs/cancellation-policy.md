# Order cancellation policy (approved by Product, v3)

1. A customer may cancel their own order until it has been shipped.
2. Support staff may cancel any order until it has been delivered.
3. Nobody else may cancel an order.
4. Orders with an unrecognized status must be rejected with an error, never silently allowed.

Refund timing for cancelled orders is defined in the payments policy and is out of scope here.
